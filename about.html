About SUJAG Education

A learning platform designed to help students and job seekers prepare for examinations through practice tests, study materials and educational resources.

Our purpose

Preparing for a recruitment test or an entry test can feel scattered. Notes are in one place, practice questions in another, and it is hard to know how you are doing. SUJAG Education brings these together in one simple website that opens quickly on a phone.

The platform is mainly built for students in Pakistan who are preparing for government job tests, university admission tests and subject exams. We want practice to be easy to start and free to use.

What you will find here

Free mock tests

Timed multiple-choice tests grouped by exam and subject. You get a score, a percentage and a full review of every answer with an explanation.

Free study notes

Short revision notes for subjects such as English, general knowledge, Pakistan Studies and computer. Read them online or download the PDF.

Government jobs, university entry tests, English, mathematics, science, computer and current affairs each have their own section so you can focus.

How it works

SUJAG Education is a static website. You do not need an account to use it. Your test progress and results are saved in your own browser, on your own device, so they are not stored on a server and may be lost if you clear your browser data.

Please note

Our practice tests and notes are prepared for learning and revision. They are not official past papers, and SUJAG Education is not affiliated with any government department, commission or testing service. Always check the official advertisement and website for exact syllabus, dates and rules before you apply.
