Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about tests, notes, books and how your results are saved.

Books are listed in the books store. Open a book, press Purchase and fill in your details to prepare an order request. At the moment ordering is not connected to an online payment system, so the request has to be sent through the contact method the site owner configures, such as WhatsApp. No payment is taken on this website.

Results are saved only in your own browser on the device you used. They are not uploaded to a server. If you clear your browser data, use a private window or switch devices, the saved results will not be available.

Notes that have a PDF show a Download PDF button on the notes page and on the note details page. Notes without a PDF can still be read online.

Your answers and your remaining time are saved in your browser, so a refresh will not lose them. The timer keeps running against the original end time, so you cannot extend it by reloading.
