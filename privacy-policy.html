Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what information SUJAG Education handles when you use this website. We have kept it plain and short because the site is simple.

No accounts, no sign-up

You do not need an account to use the site, and we do not ask for a password or payment details anywhere on it.

Information stored in your browser

To make tests work, the site saves some data in your browser using local storage on your device:

  • Your answers, current question and test end time while a test is in progress.
  • Your finished test results, including the questions and the answers you selected.
  • Your name, city and the optional contact number entered before you start.

When you submit, Sujag Education saves your name, city, score, test details, submission time and any phone number you provided in its results database. The admin panel groups submissions by test. Its result PDFs contain name, city and score. Your individual answers and explanations stay in your browser. You can remove your local history by clearing your browser's site data, or with the Clear saved results button on the results page. Contact Sujag Education to request removal of a submitted result.

Contact form and book orders

The contact form and the book order form only send information if the site owner has connected them to a messaging or email service. If a service such as WhatsApp or a form provider is connected, the details you type will be shared with that service so we can reply to you. If it is not connected, nothing is sent.

Fonts and third-party services

This website loads its text font from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser may request the font from Google, which can receive your IP address and browser details. Links to social networks and WhatsApp lead to services with their own privacy policies.

Analytics and advertising

This version of the site does not use analytics or advertising cookies. If this changes, this page will be updated.

Children

The site is meant for students and job seekers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the website grows. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page.
